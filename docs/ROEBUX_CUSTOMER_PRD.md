# RoeBux — Customer & MVP PRD

Concise product requirements for the RoeBux storefront MVP. Companion to `CLAUDE.md` and the implementation checklist. Treat conversion guidance as **directional, not rigid**.

> **Evidence status:** the customer profile below is currently a **working assumption** drawn from the founder's hunting experience and the brand brief, not yet validated by formal research. The NL-first / Germany-and-Europe-later market path is a **future hypothesis to test**, not a confirmed plan. Update this document as real customer insight is gathered; avoid treating these assumptions as established facts.

## Primary customer
Active hunters in the Netherlands, later Germany/Europe, who value preparation, order, quality and reliable gear in the field. They:
- want practical, well-designed gear that feels personal, precise and premium — not cheap mass-market accessories;
- value caliber-specific storage, clean organization and reliable field gear;
- appreciate technical detail, craftsmanship and customization;
- want gear that feels purposeful, not gimmicky;
- may pay more for a made-to-order product when the quality and use case are clear.

## Customer problem
- Cartridges and small field items are often stored in generic, cheap or messy containers.
- Hunters want gear kept organized, protected and easy to identify.
- Existing options feel too basic, too tactical, too generic, or not personal enough.
- There is room for premium, purpose-built hunting accessories that combine practical field use with customization and a strong brand feel.

## RoeBux promise
- Premium cartridge storage and field-ready hunting accessories.
- Designed by a hunter, for hunters.
- Built around precision, preparation, order, reliability and customization.
- Built with modern 3D-printed manufacturing, allowing precision, customization and continuous product improvement.
- Not ammunition sales, not firearms, not tactical/combat positioning.

## Desired brand experience
Cinematic, premium, technical, clean, calm, adventurous, respectful toward hunting and wildlife. The site should sell trust, precision and brand feeling first — a premium brand landing page with commerce integrated, not a direct-response webshop. Restraint over hype.

## MVP website goals
A visitor should, within roughly five seconds, **understand** (comprehension, not aggressive click-optimization):
1. RoeBux sells premium cartridge storage and hunting field accessories.
2. Products are customizable / made-to-order, or available in practical variants.
3. The brand is premium, technical, clean and respectful toward hunting.
4. They can shop the collection or request a custom order.

Supporting: visitors can find lead time and customization possibilities cleanly — primarily on product pages — without the homepage feeling sales-heavy.

## What the homepage should communicate
- A strong cinematic first impression and clear brand identity.
- The two product lines (premium Signature Cartridge Case; practical Field Cartridge Holder) understood at a glance.
- Why RoeBux: precision fit, made to order, field-ready design.
- That custom colors/logos/calibers are possible via request (no live configurator yet).
- A calm, controlled made-to-order craft/process story that justifies lead time and price.
- Respectful field-use tone throughout.

## What NOT to optimize aggressively yet
- No rigid primary/secondary CTA structure; CTAs exist where natural and stay understated.
- Product cards stay calm — no stacks of badges, labels or conversion widgets; lead-time/customization detail lives mainly on product pages.
- No conversion-first generic-shop styling, no discount/sale-heavy layouts, no aggressive sales language.
- No cheap-webshop feel; no dark-green hunting cliché; no tactical/gun-bro tone.

## Reference-site usage (inspiration only — never copy)
- **Blaser** — cinematic premium hero, technical/adventurous feel, transparent header.
- **KUIU** — clean shopping/product flow, technical product confidence.
- **Härkila** — premium hunting atmosphere, field imagery.
Do not copy layout, source code, text, visual assets or brand-specific patterns from any of them.
